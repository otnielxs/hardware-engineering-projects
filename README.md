## 📑 Featured Projects

### 1. [Variable Regulated DC Power Supply (3V - 12V)](./01-analog-regulated-power-supply)
- **Category:** Analog Electronics
- **Key Hardware:** Transformer 2A CT, Bridge Diode (1N4007), IC LM317 Regulator, 5kΩ Potentiometer
- **Summary:** Designed and built a linear regulated DC power supply converting 220V AC into an adjustable 3V - 12V DC output, validated with Falstad/Tinkercad simulations and physical assembly in an enclosure.
